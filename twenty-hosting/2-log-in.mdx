---
title: "Log in"
description: "Sign in to your hosted Twenty CRM for the first time and set your own password."
---

When you finish this page you are signed in to your CRM at your own address with a password only you know.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your invite email">
    Look for the email from support@madeformeai.com with your CRM address and your login details. It may be in spam.
    That email has your starter login, so keep it private or delete it once you have changed the password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your CRM address">
    Go to the address in the email on a computer. The login page appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in the first time">
    If the email gave you a starter password, enter your email address and that password.
    If the email says to set your password with a reset link, open that link and choose a password first, then sign in with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the starter password">
    Open the menu under your name, then your account settings, and change the password to one only you know.
    Do this on your first sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Google or Microsoft, if it was set up for you">
    If Dustin set up single sign-on for your business, the login page shows a **Google** or **Microsoft** button. Use the one that matches your work email. There is no separate password to keep.
    If you do not see those buttons, they were not set up for you. The email and password path above is your login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>On a shared computer, sign out when you finish, from the menu under your name.</Note>

Next: [Invite your staff](/twenty-hosting/3-invite-your-staff).

## If this fails

- You forgot your password -> use the forgot-password link on the login page and follow the email it sends. The email comes from your CRM address.
- The reset email never arrives -> check spam, then email support@madeformeai.com and ask for a new reset link.
- The Google or Microsoft button says your account is not allowed -> you signed in with a personal account instead of your work one. Sign out of that account and try again with your work email.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
