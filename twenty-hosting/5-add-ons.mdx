When you finish this page you know the three extras most businesses add to their CRM and how to ask for one. Each is set up by Dustin on request. None of them is on until you ask.

<Steps>
  <Step title="A phone line that answers and logs calls">
    A phone number with an assistant that answers, talks to the caller, takes their details, books them the same way your website does, and writes a summary of the call into the CRM against that person's record.
    To ask: email support@madeformeai.com with your business hours, the services you offer, and how you want it to sound.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automations">
    Bookings from your booking form land in the CRM as records. Follow-up emails go out on their own, keyed to a date on the record, in your words. Each email is written with you and stays in test mode until you say go.
    To ask: email support@madeformeai.com with the email you want sent, when it should send, and the address it should come from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A website form that creates a lead">
    A form on your website that creates a person in the CRM when someone fills it in, without duplicating anyone already there.
    To ask: email support@madeformeai.com with the fields the form should have and the page it should live on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Every add-on is described back to you with what it takes before anything is built. Nothing goes live to real clients until you confirm.</Note>

## If this fails

- You asked for an add-on and are not sure of the status -> reply to your original email and ask where it stands.
- An automation sends the wrong wording -> email support@madeformeai.com with the exact email you received and the wording you want.
- A lead came in twice -> email the two record names. Duplicates are meant to be caught at the door, so this is worth reporting.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
