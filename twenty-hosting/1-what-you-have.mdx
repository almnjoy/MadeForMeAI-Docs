When you finish this page you know what is yours, what Dustin maintains, and where to send a request. Your CRM is Twenty, running as your own private copy at your own web address.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Know your address">
    Your CRM lives at the web address in your welcome email. It is yours alone. No other business shares it.
    Bookmark it. Everything else on these pages starts there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know what Dustin maintains">
    **Backups:** taken every night and kept off-site, separate from the server your CRM runs on.
    **Upgrades:** new Twenty versions are tested first and applied for you. You do not install anything.
    **Attachments:** files you attach to records are stored in separate object storage, not on the CRM server.
    **Login:** if single sign-on was set up for you, the login page is maintained too. See [Log in](/twenty-hosting/2-log-in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know what you own">
    Your data is yours: every person, company, record, note and file. The workspace is in your name.
    Your staff logins and their roles are yours to decide. See [Invite your staff](/twenty-hosting/3-invite-your-staff).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know how to get your data out">
    Twenty can export the records in any list to a spreadsheet file. Open the list, open its menu, and look for the export option.
    For a full copy of everything, email support@madeformeai.com and ask for an export. You get the whole database as a file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know how to ask for a change">
    Email support@madeformeai.com. Describe what you want in plain words: the field you need, the view you want, the email that should go out.
    You get a reply that says what it takes and when it can be done. Nothing changes until you say go.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Do not change settings under **Data Model** or **Workflows** unless Dustin walked you through it. Those drive the automations. A change there can stop a reminder from sending without any error.</Note>

## If this fails

- You cannot find your CRM address -> search your email for "welcome" from support@madeformeai.com, or ask for it again.
- A file you attached will not open -> wait a minute and try again. If it still fails, email the record name and the file name.
- You asked for a change and heard nothing in two working days -> reply to your own email so it lands at the top again, and include the date you first sent it.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
