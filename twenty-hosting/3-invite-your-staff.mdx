---
title: "Invite your staff"
description: "Give each person on your team their own CRM login with the right role."
---

When you finish this page each person who works in your CRM has their own login and a role that fits what they do.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Sign in on a computer. Open **Settings**, then **Members**.
    You see everyone who has a login today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a person">
    Use the invite option and enter their work email address. They receive an email with a link to set their password and sign in.
    Invite one email per person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick their role">
    Roles were set up with you when your CRM was built. In plain terms:
    **Owner (or admin):** can change settings, edit the data model, run workflows, invite and remove people, and delete records. This is you.
    **Staff:** can see and work the records their job needs. Staff cannot change settings, and by default cannot delete records or see fields that were hidden from their role, such as money.
    If you are not sure which role a person needs, give them staff. Email support@madeformeai.com to change it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell them what to do next">
    Send them the link to [Log in](/twenty-hosting/2-log-in). Their invite email is their starting point.
    If they will use the phone app, they need their own API key made while signed in as themselves. See [Use it on your phone](/twenty-hosting/4-use-it-on-your-phone).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>Do not share one login between people. A shared login means anyone with it can delete or change anything the owner can, and you cannot tell who did what. It also means one person leaving forces everyone to change the password.</Warning>

## If this fails

- The invite email never arrives -> check their spam folder, then remove the invite and send it again to the exact work address.
- A staff member sees a field or button they should not -> their role is too broad. Email support@madeformeai.com with their name and what they should not see.
- A staff member cannot do something their job needs -> their role is too narrow. Same email, say what they need.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
