---
title: "Use it on your phone"
description: "The two ways to reach your CRM from a phone: the PocketOps app or your phone's browser."
---

When you finish this page your CRM is one tap away on your phone. The PocketOps app is built for the phone. The browser also works when you need the full CRM.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the PocketOps app">
    Follow [Install PocketOps](/pocketops/1-install), then [Connect it to your CRM](/pocketops/2-connect).
    Each person on your team connects with their own API key, made while signed in as themselves, so the app respects their role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or open the CRM in your browser">
    Go to your CRM address in your phone's browser and sign in the same way as on a computer.
    Use this for anything the app does not cover, such as settings or the data model.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If this fails

- The app is on the App Store but you have an Android phone -> use the browser path until the Android version clears review.
- The browser version is hard to use on a small screen -> the app is the better fit for day-to-day work. Use the browser for settings only.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
