A CRM built on Twenty and shaped to whatever you run: leads, pipelines, clients, subscriptions, custom fields and objects, with AI agents wired straight in. Whatever your business tracks, we model it and let the agents work it. This is the same engine behind my own OpsCanvas.

## Shaped to your business, not the other way around

Most CRMs hand you their idea of a sales process and make you live in it. This one starts from what you actually track. Clients, jobs, appointments, subscriptions, gear, whatever your world is made of becomes real objects with real fields. The pipeline stages are yours. The views are yours. The engine reads your CRM's live schema and renders around it, so when your business changes shape, the CRM follows without a rebuild.

## Agents that work the records

This is the part that makes it different. The agents don't just read your CRM, they act on it, inside guardrails you set:

- **Inline edits**: update a field from the client profile without leaving the page. Which fields are editable is an explicit allowlist, per business.
- **Action buttons**: one click runs a real workflow. Send the welcome email, kick off onboarding, fire the follow-up sequence. Each action is a defined series of steps, not an AI improvising with your client list.
- **A Client 360 view**: everything about one client on one page: record, timeline, activity, and the actions you can take.

## Client portals

Your clients get their own branded door: a portal where they log in with an emailed code, fill out the forms you assign, and everything they submit lands on their record as a proper attachment, including generated branded PDFs. No more chasing intake paperwork through email threads.

## The rest of the machine

Newsletters and client comms run from the same records, so your audience is always your actual client list, not a stale export. Intake forms feed the CRM directly. And because it pairs with [MindMap](/mindmap), you can ask questions about your pipeline in plain language and get answers as tiles on a dashboard.

## The stack

[Twenty](https://twenty.com), the open-source CRM, as the system of record. My OpsCanvas engine on top for the views, the agent wiring, and the config-driven shaping. Workflow automation handles the moving pieces like emails and sequences. It runs on your own instance with your own data.

## Status

Live. I run my own consulting business and product on this exact engine daily, and customer businesses run on their own instances.
