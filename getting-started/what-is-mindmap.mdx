description: "An AI workspace where every tool the assistant uses is something you can see and turn on or off, on a canvas and in chat."
---

MindMap is your AI workspace. You talk to one assistant, and it works across everything you have connected: email, billing, your CRM, and other apps. Every tool it uses is something you can see, click into, and turn on or off.

## One assistant, two surfaces

Chat and Canvas are not two different assistants. They are two ways to talk to the same one. Same assistant, same keys, same memory behind both.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Canvas" icon="diagram-project">
    A visual board where the apps and data the assistant works with show up as tiles you can arrange, save, and click into.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chat" icon="comments">
    A conversation where you type and the reply streams in, with results landing as cards. This is what you see on a phone.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Switch between them any time. A write you approve in Chat is the same kind of action you would approve on the Canvas.

## Every tool is something you can toggle

Nothing is hidden. Each thing the assistant can do is a node on the Canvas or a card in the marketplace. You see what is connected, you turn pieces on and off, and you click a node to open what is behind it.

## The marketplace and its four buckets

Everything the assistant can do comes from the marketplace under **Admin**. It has four buckets:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Apps" icon="grid">
    Full surfaces like Money, Sales, Morning Brief, and Chat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tools" icon="screwdriver-wrench">
    Connectors and integrations the assistant calls: CRM, email, voice, browser, storage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Skills" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Multi-step flows like Deep Research and Lead Outreach that the assistant runs end to end.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Secrets" icon="key">
    API keys and tokens, saved write-only, never shown back to the browser.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You flip a card on, and it applies on your next message. No restart. A connector hub like Zapier or Composio reaches many apps behind one key. See [Connect an app](/guides/5-connect-an-app).

## Approval-first by design

MindMap never sends or changes anything on its own.

- **Reads run on their own.** Checking a balance, reading your pipeline. Instant.
- **Writes wait for you.** Sending an email, creating a lead, moving a deal. Queued for your approval.
- **Trust Rules let you grant once.** When you are ready, approve one narrow action so it runs by itself next time. Secrets can never be auto-approved.

See [Approving what the AI does](/guides/9-approving-what-the-ai-does).
