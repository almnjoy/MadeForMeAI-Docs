The flagship. MindMap is one AI workspace where your agent can use every tool you connect: your CRM, email, calendar, code repos, the web, and more. You work two ways. **Canvas** lays answers out as tiles you can drag, arrange, and save into dashboards. **Chat** is a plain linear thread, the default on phones. Same agent, same keys, same memory behind both.

## Why it exists

I wanted one place where an agent could actually run a business without me babysitting every step or losing control of what it changes. The answer is a hard split: reads run the moment you ask, and every write queues behind an approval gate. Nothing sends, posts, or changes a record until you click approve. For the writes you repeat and trust, a Trust Rule pre-approves that narrow case so you stop clicking for it.

## What you get

Your own workspace, not a seat on someone else's. Each MindMap runs as its own isolated instance with its own login, its own keys, and its own memory. Inside it:

- **The marketplace**: Apps, Tools, Skills, and Secrets. Flip a feature on, add its key, and the agent can use it.
- **Dashboards**: turn Canvas answers into tiles and save them as views you come back to every morning.
- **CRM and clients**: pipelines, client profiles, and agent actions wired to your real records.
- **Memory**: the agent remembers who you are, what you run, and what you told it last week.
