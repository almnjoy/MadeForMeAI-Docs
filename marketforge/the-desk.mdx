One screen, seven tabs. Here is what each one is for.

## OVERVIEW

Your account at a glance: equity, cash, positions, day P/L, drawdown, and the
bankroll the bot is allowed to size against. Below that, a candlestick chart for any
symbol with volume, a price axis, and the last-price line, plus recent headlines for
that ticker. Positions and recent orders sit on the right, each with a Trade button.

Type any symbol into the chart box to switch it.

## CATALYST_RADAR

The core. Each card is a stock that moved, with:

- The **verified** percentage move. The screener's number is never trusted directly:
  it is recomputed from real daily bars and the live tape, and if the real move does
  not clear the bar, the card is dropped entirely.
- A **conviction score** (0-100) and a **signal** or **noise** verdict from the LLM,
  with a one-line reason.
- **Live "now versus alert"** so you can see whether the move followed through or
  faded since the bot flagged it.
- The source headline, the catalyst type, and a discipline note.

**Re-scan** forces a fresh scan; the timestamp beside it tells you how stale the
current one is.

## RETAIL_RADAR

What Reddit is pushing right now, from the hot pages of r/wallstreetbets,
r/swingtrading, and r/stocks. Tickers are extracted, validated as real tradeable
symbols, and ranked by how much they are being talked about, with links to the actual
posts. The same buzz is fed to the catalyst radar's scorer, so a name with news *and*
chatter reads differently than one with only chatter.

## WORKBENCH

<Frame caption="WORKBENCH - panels the copilot writes appear live">
  <img src="/images/marketforge/workbench.png" alt="Market Forge workbench" />
</Frame>

The copilot's canvas. Every HTML file in the `panels/` folder renders here as a live
card, and the page re-reads that folder every couple of seconds. So when you ask for
a board, you watch it appear.

- Drag any card's corner to resize it; your sizes are remembered.
- **Save board** freezes the current set under a name; **Load** brings it back (and
  auto-saves whatever was there first).
- Panels can request full width, so "tomorrow's plan" comes back as one proper board
  rather than a wall of tiles.

## RULES

The agent's plan, in plain language: the whole pipeline from screener to verified
move to scoring to gates to a trailing-stop entry, followed by every live setting the
engine is running with.

**Click any setting** and it drafts a question to the copilot about that knob - what
it controls, the trade-off of changing it, and how to apply it. Your own
training-wheels document sits beside it.

## JOURNAL

The decision log. Every scan, chat, board, and order is recorded with a timestamp.
Pick a day and you see the timeline; press **Replay this day** and the copilot
reconstructs it: what fired, what you took, what you skipped, and one lesson about
your process.

It is git history for your trading decisions, and it is worth more than the P/L
number after a few weeks.

## COPILOT

<Frame caption="COPILOT - chat or voice, with the bridge answering automatically">
  <img src="/images/marketforge/copilot.png" alt="Market Forge copilot" />
</Frame>

Chat with your agent. Type or talk; replies render here and are spoken aloud. While
it works you see its real steps ("Write · panels/10-plan.html"), and a **Stop** button
kills the turn and silences the voice instantly.

See [the copilot](/marketforge/copilot) for the full picture.

## Things that are easy to miss

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ctrl+K opens a command palette" icon="keyboard">
    Type `chart amd`, `trade nvda`, `re-scan`, `save board`, `clear board`,
    `morning brief`, `hot mic`, or a saved board's name. Anything the palette does not
    recognise gets sent to the copilot as a message - so it is one box for the whole
    app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The hot mic works from any tab" icon="microphone">
    The button in the top bar keeps listening while you look at charts. It ignores the
    copilot's own voice so it does not talk to itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The memory drawer is your plan" icon="brain">
    The brain icon opens `memory.md`: your standing orders, honored on every copilot
    turn. Saying "remember I don't touch biotech" writes a line there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The ticker tape pauses" icon="pause">
    Hover to pause it, click to pin the pause. Each ticker is tinted by the size of
    its move, so the tape doubles as a heat strip.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The market clock knows the session" icon="clock">
    Top right: pre-market, open with a countdown to the close, after-hours, or closed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
