Market Forge does not try to be a charting package. TradingView is thousands of
engineer-years ahead and it is free. What Market Forge adds is the ability to
**drive** it and to **read** it.

<Note>
Entirely optional. Skip this page and everything else still works.
</Note>

## What you get

Say *"pull up AMWL on the daily"* and your actual TradingView chart moves. Ask
what the chart looks like, and the desk captures it to a PNG that an AI agent can
open and describe.

That second half is the interesting one. It turns a question you can only answer
with your eyes into **a file**, which any image-capable agent can read.

## Setup

1. Have TradingView open in a browser (a free account is fine).
2. Run **`run-tradingview.bat`**. It opens Chrome with the DevTools protocol on
   port 9222, using its own profile.
3. Log in to TradingView once in that window.

<Warning>
The separate Chrome profile is load-bearing. Without it, Chrome hands the URL to
your already-running browser and silently ignores the debug flag, and nothing
works with no error to explain why.
</Warning>

**The TradingView desktop app will not work.** It ships from the Microsoft Store
as a packaged app, so it cannot accept the debugging flag and its install
directory is locked. Use the browser.

## Using it

Just talk to the copilot:

```text
pull up AMWL on the daily
show me SPY on the 15 minute
chart NVDA and tell me what you see
```

Or call it directly:

| | |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/tv/open` | `{symbol, interval}` - `1m 5m 15m 30m 1h 4h 1d 1w` |
| `POST /api/tv/shot` | capture to `tv-shots/`, returns a `web_path` |
| `GET /api/tv/status` | is the debug browser up, is TradingView open |

Captures are servable, so a panel can show one next to its notes:

```html
<img src="/api/shot?name=tv-20260806-111920.png">
```

## What it deliberately does not do

No drawing tools, no indicator manipulation, no reading TradingView's internal
chart state. Symbol and interval travel in the URL, which is public and stable.
Everything beyond that would mean reverse-engineering their internals, which
breaks on their next release and is not a foundation worth building on.

## Going the other way

TradingView alerts can POST to a webhook. Pointing them at Market Forge would make
their alert engine another radar input, running through the same verification and
the same risk gates as everything else. Their charting, your discipline. Not built
yet, but the shape is right.
