Market Forge does not have an AI feature bolted on. It has a **seat**, and any capable
coding agent can sit in it.

## How it actually works

The desk and the agent talk through plain files in the project folder. No API, no
plugin, no SDK.

```
you ──voice/chat──> dashboard ──HTTP──> bot engine (scanner, gates, orders)
        │               │
        │               ├── panels/*.html    the agent writes files here,
        └── AI agent ───┼── chat-*.jsonl     the dashboard renders them live
                        ├── memory.md
                        └── journal.jsonl
```

That is the whole contract. It is why the copilot is not locked to one vendor: if your
agent can read and write files and make local HTTP calls, it can drive this desk.

## The two lanes

  <Card title="Your terminal agent" icon="terminal">

  </Card>

Both read `CLAUDE.md`, which is the copilot's brief: the API cheat sheet, the panel
format, and the hard rules.

## Talking to it

- **Type** in the COPILOT tab.
- **Push to talk** with the mic button.
- **Hot mic** from the top bar keeps listening while you work in other tabs, and
  ignores the copilot's own speech so it does not loop.
- Replies are **spoken aloud**. With [Voicebox](https://voicebox.sh/) running you
  get a natural voice; otherwise the browser reads it.

While a turn runs you see its real tool steps and elapsed time, plus a **Stop** button
that kills the turn and silences the audio immediately.

## Asking for boards

The Workbench is the copilot's canvas. Useful things to say:

<CodeGroup>
```text Morning
Build the morning brief as one full-width board: market state, my account,
top radar signals with your read, reddit overlap, and the plan for today.
```

```text Research
Pull the newest catalyst - chart, news links, reddit chatter, and your read
on whether it is chaseable.
```

```text Watchlist
Make me a watchlist board for AMD, NVDA and SPY with candles and the levels
that matter.
```

```text Cleanup
Clear the board and build tomorrow's plan as one page.
```
</CodeGroup>

Panels appear within a couple of seconds of being written. Ask for a change and watch
it transform.

## Memory: teaching it your plan

The brain icon in the top bar opens `memory.md` - your standing orders. Example:

```md
- Max $50 per trade while learning
- Every entry gets an exit: trailing stop, 10% default
- Don't chase IPOs or sub-$3 low-float spikers
- Prefer earnings-driven catalysts over vague momentum
- One idea at a time; no revenge trades
```

Those lines are injected into **every** copilot turn. Say "remember I only trade the
first two hours" and it appends the rule itself.

<Note>
This file is the fastest way to make the desk yours. When someone hands Market Forge
to a friend, rewriting `memory.md` is most of the personalisation.
</Note>

## Replay: reviewing process, not P/L

Everything that happens is logged to `journal.jsonl`. In the JOURNAL tab, pick a day
and press **Replay this day**. The copilot reads the log, cross-checks your orders,
and reconstructs the session: what the radar fired, what you took, what you passed on,
how it ended, and one lesson about how you decided.

Most trading journals record outcomes. This one records decisions, which is the part
you can actually improve.

## The hard rules

The copilot's brief forbids it from placing trades or changing risk settings without
your explicit instruction in that conversation. The trade ticket and your own terminal
session are the execution lanes - the chat lane is for research and building.

Trust but verify: the journal records every order, and the RULES tab always shows the
settings the engine is actually running.
