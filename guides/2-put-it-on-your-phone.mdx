---
title: "Put it on your phone"
description: "Add MindMap to your phone's home screen so it opens full screen like an app."
---

When you finish this page MindMap has an icon on your phone's home screen and opens full screen, on the same account and data you use on a computer. There is nothing to download from an app store.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone or iPad">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open MindMap in Safari">
        Go to madeformeai.com in **Safari** and sign in.
        Chrome on iPhone cannot install it. Use Safari for this part.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Share">
        Tap the **Share** button, the box with an arrow pointing up.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Add to Home Screen">
        Scroll the share menu until you find **Add to Home Screen**, then tap it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Add">
        Keep or change the name, then tap **Add** in the top corner.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open MindMap in Chrome">
        Go to madeformeai.com in **Chrome** and sign in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the menu">
        Tap the **menu** (the three dots) in the top corner.
        Some phones show an install banner at the bottom instead. That works too.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Install app">
        Tap **Install app**. Some phones label it **Add to Home screen**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Tap **Add** or **Install** to confirm.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Tap the new icon and MindMap opens full screen with no browser bars. You land on **Chat**, and the sidebar folds into a menu button. Tap any result card to open it full screen, then close it to go back to the conversation.

<Note>
The installed app is the live site, so it updates on its own. You will not find MindMap in the App Store or Google Play. If you also use Twenty CRM, the PocketOps app is separate, see [PocketOps](/pocketops/1-install).
</Note>

## If this fails

- You cannot find Add to Home Screen on iPhone -> you are probably in Chrome. Open madeformeai.com in Safari and try again.
- The app opens but shows nothing -> you need an internet connection. There is no offline mode.
- The Canvas board looks cramped -> it is built for a larger screen. Use Chat on the phone and the Canvas on a tablet or computer.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
