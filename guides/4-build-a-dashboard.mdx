---
title: "Build a dashboard"
description: "Ask for a dashboard in plain words, approve it once, and get a page of live tiles."
---

When you finish this page you have a dashboard the assistant built for you: a page of tiles such as a number, a chart, a table, or a short note, and you know how to change it by asking.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Dashboards">
    Open **Admin**, find the **Dashboards** card, and flip **Enable** on.
    It applies on your next message. No key to paste.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for the board in Chat">
    Open **Chat** and describe what you want on it.
    Try: "Build me a dashboard of this week's leads, revenue, and top opportunities."
    The assistant picks the tiles that fit and arranges them into one page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve it">
    Building a dashboard is a write, so the whole board arrives as one approval card. Click **Approve** and all the tiles land together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboards page">
    The **Dashboards** page lists every board you have built. Open one, or ask "show me my dashboards."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it by asking">
    Name the board you mean. Each change queues its own approval.
    "Add a KPI for my newest credit card balance to the money page."
    "Change the revenue chart on my sales board to a bar chart."
    "Remove the table from the leads board."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate a tile">
    Every tile has a thumbs up and a thumbs down. Tap one to mark whether it was useful. The vote goes to the audit log so you can look back later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A tile is live or a snapshot. A live tile re-fetches its data every time you open the board, but only when the source behind it is set up in its own marketplace card first: Stripe or Plaid for money tiles, Twenty for pipeline tiles. Without that source the assistant fills the tile with static values. Ask for live tiles when you want them current.
</Note>

The assistant never writes code to build a board. It picks from a fixed catalog of tile types (KPI, chart, table, doc), so what it builds is always something MindMap already knows how to draw. The **Money** page is itself a live board named money, so "edit the money page" edits that board.

## If this fails

- The assistant says it cannot build dashboards -> the Dashboards card is off. Turn it on in **Admin** and ask again.
- A tile shows a stale or placeholder number -> connect the source behind it (Stripe, Plaid, or Twenty) in **Admin**, then ask for a live tile.
- The board never appears -> look for the approval card and click **Approve**. Nothing lands until you do.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
