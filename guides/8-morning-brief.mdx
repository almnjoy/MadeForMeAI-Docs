---
title: "Morning brief"
description: "A short daily summary of what matters today, at the top of your Overview."
---

When you finish this page a short daily standup sits at the top of your **Overview**, built from whatever you have connected: your pipeline, your newest lead, cash from Stripe, bank balances from Plaid, tasks, your calendar, and saved files.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Morning Brief card">
    Open **Admin** and click the **Morning Brief** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flip Enable on">
    It applies on your next message. There is no key or URL for the brief itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to your Overview">
    The brief shows at the top. A section with no data behind it is skipped, so you only see what you have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Regenerate">
    The brief is a snapshot from the moment it was made. Click **Regenerate** any time to rebuild it with current numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shape it in Chat">
    Tell the assistant how you want it. These change your brief setup and stick between generations.
    "Lead my morning brief with a greeting and put tasks first."
    "Add a custom brief source that summarizes my unread email."
    "Add a closing note that reminds me to log my hours."
    "Reset my morning brief to default."
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The more apps you connect, the more sections it can show. The cash line needs Stripe, the bank line needs Plaid, and the pipeline line needs your CRM. See [Money](/guides/7-money) and [Connect your CRM](/guides/6-connect-your-crm).
</Note>

The brief only reads. It will tell you a deal went cold or a balance is low, but it never emails anyone, changes a record, or moves money. Custom sources are read-only summaries too, and you can add a few of them.

## If this fails

- A section is missing -> the app behind it is not connected, or it has no data. Connect it in **Admin**, then click **Regenerate**.
- The numbers look old -> click **Regenerate**. The brief holds the snapshot from its last run.
- The brief does not show on the Overview -> the Morning Brief card is off. Flip **Enable** on and send one message.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
