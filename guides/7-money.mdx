When you finish this page the **Money** page shows your Stripe revenue, your bank, credit, and investment balances, and a net worth number, and the assistant can answer "how much cash do I have." Everything here is read-only. Nothing moves money.

Stripe and Plaid are independent. Set up one or both. Stripe needs a key from you. Plaid is managed for you, so there are no Plaid keys to enter. You only link your bank.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a read-only Stripe key">
    In Stripe, open **Developers**, then **API keys**. Create a **restricted key** with read access only: Balance read, Charges read, Payouts read. A read-only key can never move money.
    Skip this step if you only want bank balances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Money card">
    In MindMap, open **Admin** and click the **Money** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the Stripe key and Save">
    Paste the key into the **Stripe secret key** box and click **Save**. The box is write-only. The key is never shown back to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flip Enable on">
    It applies on your next message. No restart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a bank">
    Open the **Money** page and click **Connect a bank**. Log in to your bank in the window that opens. Repeat for each bank you want to see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the assistant">
    In **Chat**, try "what's my cash position," "what's my net worth," or "what's pending in Stripe." Whichever side you set up is what comes back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Net worth is cash plus investments minus credit, across every bank you linked. Balances are cached, not live to the second. They are fine for a cash position, not for trading numbers.
</Note>

The Money page is a live board, so its numbers refresh every time you open it. Ask "show me the money tile" to drop the same numbers on the Canvas.

## If this fails

- Plaid shows nothing -> you have not linked a bank yet. Open the **Money** page and click **Connect a bank**.
- Stripe shows nothing -> the key is missing or wrong. Paste it into the Money card again and click **Save**.
- Transactions are empty for one account -> that bank's link does not include transactions. Balances still work.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
