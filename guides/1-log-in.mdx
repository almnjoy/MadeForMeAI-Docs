---
title: "Log in"
description: "Sign in to MindMap and land on your dashboard."
---

When you finish this page you are signed in to MindMap and looking at your dashboard. You do not set a separate MindMap password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open madeformeai.com">
    Go to [madeformeai.com](https://madeformeai.com) in your browser.
    If you are signed out, the dashboard sends you straight to the login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how you want to sign in">
    Choose **Google**, **GitHub**, **Microsoft**, or **Discord** to sign in with an account you already have.
    Or type your email address and choose the email option to get a magic link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the sign-in">
    With Google, GitHub, Microsoft, or Discord, approve the sign-in when that service asks.
    With email, open the message from MindMap and click the link inside. That link signs you in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your dashboard">
    After sign-in you return to madeformeai.com on your dashboard. **Chat** is where you talk to the assistant. On a phone, Chat is the first thing you see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bookmark it">
    Save madeformeai.com in your browser so you can get back in one click. Your browser keeps the session, so you stay signed in on that device until it expires or you sign out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Sign out">
    Use **Sign out** in the header. It ends your MindMap session on that device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in again the same way">
    Next time, pick the same Google, GitHub, Microsoft, or Discord account, or request a new magic link. Your memory and files live in your workspace, so a new device shows the same workspace after you sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>On a shared or public computer, sign out when you finish.</Note>

## If this fails

- The page bounces you back to the login page -> close the tab, open a new one, and go to madeformeai.com again. A half-finished session sometimes causes the loop.
- The login page loads but nothing happens after you sign in -> use a normal browser window, not private or incognito. MindMap needs cookies to hold your session.
- The magic link email never arrives -> check your spam folder, then request a new link from the login page.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
