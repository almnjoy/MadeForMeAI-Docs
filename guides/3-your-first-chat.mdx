title: "Your first chat"
description: "Ask the assistant something in plain words and read the reply."
---

When you finish this page you have asked the assistant a question, read its reply, and seen what a result card and an approval look like.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chat">
    Sign in at madeformeai.com and open the **Chat** tab. On a phone, Chat is the first thing you see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a plain sentence">
    You do not need commands or special prompts. Give the assistant something specific to do.
    Try: "What's my newest lead?", "Chart last quarter's revenue", or "Summarize my unread email from this week."
    A vague ask gets a vague answer. "Draft a follow-up email to the client who went quiet two weeks ago" gets something you can use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the reply stream in">
    The answer appears word by word. Small chips under the reply show which tools the assistant used.
    If it pauses on a chip, it is working, not stuck. Give it 10 to 30 seconds. See [Tool calls](/understanding/tool-calls).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a result card">
    A chart, a document, a search, or a lookup lands as a card under the reply. Click the card to open it in a wider side panel.
    On the Canvas, the same results land as tiles you can drag and arrange.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a follow-up chip, or type the next thing">
    After a reply the assistant offers a likely next step as a chip. Click it, or ignore it and type your own request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve your first write">
    Ask for something that changes the outside world, like "draft and send a follow-up to them." The assistant prepares the action and shows it to you before anything happens.
    Click **Approve** to send it or **Reject** to drop it. See [Approving what the AI does](/guides/9-approving-what-the-ai-does).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell it something to remember">
    Say "Remember that my business partner is named Kim." The assistant keeps it and uses it in later conversations. See [How memory works](/understanding/how-memory-works).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Your conversation stays put when you move to another tab and come back. Chat history is saved in the browser you are using, so another device or browser starts a fresh conversation. Your saved memory is shared across all of them.
</Note>

<Warning>**Clear** wipes the current conversation. There is no undo.</Warning>

## If this fails

- The assistant paused and nothing has happened for over a minute -> refresh the page and send the message again.
- It looks finished but the email or record never went out -> look for a pending approval and click **Approve**.
- The reply cut off partway -> type "continue" and it picks up where it left off.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
