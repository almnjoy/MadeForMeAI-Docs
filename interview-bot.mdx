A mock-interview agent that runs the room over Webex, asks real questions for the role, and scores your answers. Practice the actual interview, not a checklist, and get feedback you can act on.

## It runs the room

You join a Webex call and the bot plays interviewer. It greets you, sets the frame, asks the first question, and then does the thing static question lists can't: it listens to your actual answer and follows up on it. Vague on a detail? It probes. Strong answer? It escalates. The pressure is real because the conversation is real.

## Questions for the role, not for everyone

The question sets are built per role. A network engineer interview digs into routing decisions, failure stories, and design trade-offs. The bot pulls from the right set and calibrates depth to the seniority you're aiming at, so you rehearse the interview you'll actually face.

## Scored, not just survived

After the session you get more than a feeling:

- **Per-answer scoring** against a rubric: substance, structure, and whether you actually answered the question asked.
- **Pattern feedback**: the habits that repeat across answers, like burying the lede or never quantifying results.
- **A fix list**: the two or three changes that would most improve the next run, so practice has a direction.

## Why voice, why Webex

Because interviews are a voice medium and most prep isn't. Typing answers into a chat window trains the wrong muscle. Talking under light pressure, with silence after your answer and a follow-up you didn't script, is the skill itself. Webex is where a lot of real technical interviews happen anyway, so the rehearsal matches the venue.

## The stack

A voice rig on Webex, persona agents playing interviewer, and a scoring pass over the transcript after the session.

## Status

A working rig, with interviewer personas for network and IT roles first because that's the world I hire-tested it against. New roles are a question set away.
