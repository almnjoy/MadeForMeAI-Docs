## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the page">
    Plain language. "A landing page for my lawn care business, friendly, three services, a call button." The more specific the brief, the sharper the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a style">
    A grid of style presets, each a full design direction: from clean and corporate to warm and editorial to full hacker terminal. These come from my own design token engine, the same system behind my sites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and download">
    The page renders live. Like it, download it. Want different, tweak the brief or flip the style and go again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you actually get

One self-contained HTML file. All the styling lives inside it, with web fonts as the only outside dependency. That matters because a single file with no build step and no framework runs anywhere: drop it on any host, hand it to whoever manages your domain, or open it straight from your desktop. Nothing to install, nothing to maintain, nothing to break in a year.

## What it's for

A landing page before the "real site" exists. An event page. A one-page portfolio or link hub. A quick mock to show a client what a direction feels like before anyone commits to a project. Anywhere the answer is "I need one good page, today."
