Chat a trip through and get a real, shareable itinerary page: day-by-day plan, live map, food, costs. Road trips, train trips, flights, weekends away.

## How it works

Open it from the Tools tab and talk like you're texting a friend who plans well. Where you're going, when, who's coming, what kind of trip it is. It asks for what it needs, and it doesn't stall waiting for perfection: give it what you have and it builds with that.

The finish line isn't a wall of chat text. When it has enough, it **builds an actual page** for your trip and hands you the link.

## The page you get

- **Day-by-day plan**: each day laid out with the stops, times, and the why.
- **A live map**: your stops pinned on an embedded Google map, so the route is a picture instead of a paragraph.
- **Food**: real suggestions near where you'll actually be.
- **Costs**: rough numbers where they matter, so nobody's surprised.

The link is shareable. Send it to the group chat and everyone sees the same plan, no app required.

## Keep talking to it

The page isn't frozen. Come back, tell it "swap day two, we lost the morning" or "add a barbecue stop between those cities," and it rebuilds. Planning stays a conversation instead of a document you dread reopening.
