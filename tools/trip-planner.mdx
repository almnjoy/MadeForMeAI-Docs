When you are done, you have a link to a trip page: a day-by-day plan, a live map of
your stops, food suggestions near each stop, and rough costs. Anyone with the link
sees the same plan, no app required.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tools tab on your dashboard and pick Trip Planner">
    It opens as a chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type where you are going, when, who is coming, and what kind of trip it is">
    Road trip, train trip, flights, a weekend away. Give it what you have. It asks
    for anything else it needs and builds with what you give it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer its questions until it hands you the link">
    When it has enough, it builds the trip page and gives you the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link and share it">
    The page has the day-by-day plan with stops, times and the reason for each, your
    stops pinned on an embedded Google map, food suggestions near where you will be,
    and rough costs where they matter. Send the link to the group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back and change it">
    Return to the same chat and tell it what changed, for example **swap day two, we
    lost the morning** or **add a barbecue stop between those cities**. It rebuilds
    the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If this fails

- The chat keeps asking questions and never builds -> give it the dates and the starting point in one message, then say **build the page**.
- The link opens a page that is missing a day or a stop -> go back to the chat, say what is missing, and it rebuilds.
- The map is blank -> reload the page once; if it stays blank, the stops may not have exact places yet, so name the towns or addresses in the chat.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
