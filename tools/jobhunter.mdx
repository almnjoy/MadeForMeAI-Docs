---
title: "Score a job posting"
description: "Paste a posting and your background to get a fit score, gaps, tailored bullets and likely questions"
---

When you are done, you have a report on one job: a fit score out of 100, where you
are strong, the gaps and how to frame them, resume bullets in the posting's own
language, and eight likely interview questions.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tools tab on your dashboard and pick JobHunter">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the job posting">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your background">
    Resume text, a LinkedIn blurb, or a plain description of what you have done.
    There is no account linking and no resume upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it and read the report">
    - **Fit score out of 100** with a one-line verdict. It is blunt on purpose.
    - **Where you are strong**: the real matches between your background and this
      posting.
    - **Gaps and how to frame them**: what is missing, each with an honest framing
      line. It does not suggest lying.
    - **Tailored resume bullets**: your experience rewritten in the posting's
      language, quantified where your background supports it. It does not invent
      experience.
    - **Likely interview questions**: eight questions for this role, each with a
      pointer on how to answer from your background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy what you need, then close the tab">
    Nothing is stored. Once the tab is closed, the report is gone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Every claim in the report is grounded in what you pasted. The goal is the strongest
true version of your case.
</Note>

## If this fails

- The score is low and the reasons are vague -> paste more of your background, with numbers where you have them, and run it again.
- The bullets do not sound like you -> paste your own resume text instead of a summary; it rewrites what you give it.
- You closed the tab and lost the report -> run it again with the same two pastes; nothing was saved.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
