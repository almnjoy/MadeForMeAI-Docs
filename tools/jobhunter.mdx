Paste a job posting and your background: get a blunt fit score, honest gap framing, tailored resume bullets, and the questions they'll probably ask. Score it, frame it, land it.

## What you feed it

Two pastes. The job posting, and your background: resume text, a LinkedIn blurb, or just describe what you've done in plain words. No account linking, no resume upload ceremony.

## What you get back

- **A fit score out of 100** with a one-line verdict. Blunt on purpose: a 61 that tells you why beats a polite maybe.
- **Where you're strong**: the real matches between your background and this posting, grounded in what you actually wrote.
- **Gaps and how to frame them**: what's missing, each with an honest framing line. It will never suggest lying, it shows you the true story worth telling.
- **Tailored resume bullets**: your experience rewritten in the posting's own language, strong verbs, quantified where your background supports it. It never invents experience you don't have.
- **Likely interview questions**: eight questions this specific role would ask, each with a pointer on how to answer from your background.

## The honesty design

Every claim is grounded in what you pasted. No invented achievements, no keyword-stuffed fiction that falls apart in the first interview. The goal is the strongest true version of your case, because that's the version you can defend in the room.

## Privacy

Nothing is stored. Paste, get your report, close the tab, it's gone.
