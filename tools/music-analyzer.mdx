When you are done, the free Claude Desktop app on your computer can summarize your
taste, build playlists that flow, and match music to a mood, from your own Spotify,
SoundCloud, and local library. Your listening data stays on your machine.

<Note>
This one is different from the other tools. It does not run on our servers. It is a
recipe you set up once in Claude Desktop, and then it is yours.
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tools tab on your dashboard">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Under Claude Desktop Ideas, pick Music Analyzer and click Get the recipe">
    The recipe builder opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the recipe">
    It tells you what to install (the free Claude Desktop app), what to connect for
    your setup (Spotify, SoundCloud, or local files), and the prompt that turns
    Claude Desktop into your analyzer. Setup takes a few minutes, once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask it for what you want">
    - **Taste summary**: what you listen to and the patterns in your library.
    - **Playlists that flow**: sequenced sets, not a shuffled pile of keyword matches.
    - **Mood matching**: for example, focus with no lyrics for 45 minutes, or windows
      down at golden hour, from your own collection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If this fails

- Claude Desktop does not see your music -> go back to the recipe and redo the connect step for the service you use; the connection has to be made inside Claude Desktop, not on the dashboard.
- Playlists come back generic -> tell it which artists or playlists to read first, then ask again.
- The recipe page will not open -> reload the dashboard and open the Tools tab again.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
