Summarize your music taste, build playlists that actually flow, and match music to any mood, from your real Spotify, SoundCloud, and local library. This one is different from the other tools: it's a **Claude Desktop Idea**.

## Not hosted here, and that's the point

The Music Analyzer doesn't run on my servers. It runs on **your** machine, inside the free Claude Desktop app, against your own music accounts and files. I hand you the setup and the prompt; your listening data never leaves your hands. You get the capability without shipping your library or your accounts to anyone, me included.

## What it does once it's set up

- **Taste summary**: what you actually listen to, patterns you didn't notice, the honest shape of your library.
- **Playlists that flow**: sequenced sets that move like a good DJ hour, instead of a shuffled pile of songs that individually match a keyword.
- **Mood matching**: "focus, no lyrics, 45 minutes" or "windows down, golden hour" turned into music from your own collection.

## How to get it

On your dashboard's Tools tab, under **Claude Desktop Ideas**, hit "Get the recipe." The recipe builder walks you through it: what to install (the free Claude Desktop app), what to connect for your setup, and the prompt that turns it into your analyzer. Setup is a one-time few minutes, and then it's just yours.

## Why this model exists

Some tools belong on a server. Personal-data tools mostly don't. Claude Desktop Ideas is the shelf for builds where the right answer is "run it yourself, keep your data": the recipe is the product, and more of them are coming.
