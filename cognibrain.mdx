A research assistant that reads across your sources, synthesizes what matters, and remembers it so you are not starting from zero every time. Built for the way you actually dig into a topic.

## The problem it exists for

Real research doesn't happen in one sitting. You dig into a topic, build context, reach a partial answer, and then life interrupts. Two weeks later the tabs are gone, the notes are scattered, and the context you paid for with hours of reading has evaporated. Most AI tools make this worse: every chat starts from zero, and yesterday's synthesis is buried in a scroll you'll never reread.

## What it does instead

- **Reads across sources**: documents, links, and notes you feed it, worked through as a set instead of one at a time.
- **Synthesizes with receipts**: the output is a distilled answer that points back at where each claim came from, so you can check the load-bearing parts instead of trusting a vibe.
- **Remembers on purpose**: what it learns gets filed into persistent memory organized by topic, not lost in chat history. The next session starts warm: it knows what you covered, what you concluded, and what was still open.

## The compounding effect

This is the part that matters. A research assistant with memory gets more useful the longer you use it, because every session builds on the last. Ask a question next month and it answers in the context of everything you've already worked through together, flags what changed since, and picks up the open threads instead of re-answering the solved ones.
