This page lists what MindMap does as soon as you sign in, what needs a connector turned on first, and what it cannot do.

## Works right now, no setup needed

**Writing and editing**
- Draft emails, messages, proposals, social posts, bios, cover letters
- Edit and improve writing you paste in (tone, length, clarity)
- Translate to other languages
- Write in a specific voice or style

**Research and information**
- Search the web and summarize what it finds
- Answer questions on any topic
- Compare options and give you a recommendation
- Summarize long documents you upload

**Planning and thinking**
- Build a week-by-week plan for a project or goal
- Help you prioritize when you have too much going on
- Think through a decision with you
- Brainstorm ideas, names, angles, strategies

**Everyday tasks**
- Draft a response to a hard email or message
- Write a review, complaint, or feedback message
- Help you figure out what to say in a tricky situation
- Put together a checklist, template, or script

**Files you upload**
- Read and summarize PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets
- Pull key information out of documents
- Answer questions about a specific file

<Note>
Reads run on their own. Anything that changes something outside MindMap (sending an email, creating a file in a connected app) pauses and waits for your approval first. See [Tool calls](/understanding/tool-calls).
</Note>

## Works once a connector is on

A connector is turned on in the marketplace under **Admin** before MindMap can use it. The marketplace has four buckets: Apps, Tools, Skills, and Secrets. A connector usually needs a key or token saved under Secrets. See [Connect an app](/guides/5-connect-an-app).

**Email** (Gmail or Microsoft 365). Read, draft, and reply to email. Ask "check my inbox and summarize anything important" or "reply to the email from Kim about the invoice." Sends queue for your approval.

**Google Drive.** Read, create, and organize files in your Drive. Reads run on their own. Anything that creates or changes a file queues for approval.

**Calendar.** Check your schedule and add events. Adding an event queues for approval.

**CRM and more.** Connectors for tools like a CRM, GitHub, and others show up in the marketplace too. See [Connect your CRM](/guides/6-connect-your-crm).

## Things people try that do not work

**Reach into files on your computer.** MindMap runs in the cloud, not on your laptop. It cannot see your desktop, your Downloads folder, or any local file. Upload a file into the chat and it can read it. See [Cloud, not local](/understanding/cloud-not-local).

**Place phone calls.** Voice calling needs a separate connector and is off unless it has been set up for your account.

## Things people do with it every day

- "What was that idea I had last week about [topic]?" It knows, because you told it.
- "Summarize my last 10 emails and tell me what needs a response." Works once email is connected.
- "I have a job interview tomorrow at [company]. Help me prepare." Web research plus coaching in one ask.
- "Write a response to this negative review." Paste the review, get a draft.
- "Build me a content calendar for my Instagram for the next month."
- "I have $500 to spend on ads. Walk me through how to use it."

The fastest way to learn what MindMap can do is to ask it. Start with "what can you help me with?"
