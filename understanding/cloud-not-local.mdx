MindMap runs on a private server in the cloud, not on your computer. You reach it through your browser. This is the thing that trips people up most, so here it is in one place.

<Warning>
Closing your laptop or your browser tab does not stop MindMap. Your data lives in your workspace, not on your machine. To reach it, open your browser and sign in.
</Warning>

## What it cannot see

Because it runs somewhere else, MindMap cannot see:

- Files on your desktop
- Your Downloads folder
- Photos on your phone
- Anything stored only on your device
- Your browser history or open tabs

When people say "connect to my folder" or "access my files," they usually mean files on their own computer. MindMap cannot reach those. It is the same reason you cannot see your home computer's files from your phone unless you set up cloud sync first.

## What to do instead

**Upload files into the chat.** Click the attachment button in the chat window and send the file. MindMap reads it, summarizes it, pulls out data, and answers questions about it. This works for PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets, images, and more.

**Connect cloud storage.** If your files already live in something like Google Drive, that connector can be turned on in the marketplace under **Admin**, and MindMap can read your files from there. See [Connect an app](/guides/5-connect-an-app).

**Connect email.** Once an email connector is on, MindMap can read and reply to email for you. Reads run on their own. Sends queue for your approval.

## A way to picture it

Imagine an assistant who works from an office across town. They can only work with what you send them. You can email them files, give them access to your cloud storage, or share a document. They cannot walk into your house and open your filing cabinet.

MindMap is that assistant. It works from its own private office and only touches what you give it access to.

## What "the cloud" means

"The cloud" means computers connected to the internet and run by someone else. Gmail, Google Drive, iCloud, and Dropbox all work this way. MindMap is the same idea. It runs on a machine that is always on and always connected, and you reach it through your browser. Your MindMap server is private and dedicated to your workspace.
