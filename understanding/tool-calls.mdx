Sometimes you ask MindMap something and it does not reply right away. It pauses and shows something like:

> _Searching the web..._
> _Reading file..._
> _Creating document..._

That is a **tool call**. It means MindMap is doing something, not only writing text.

<Info>
A pause means MindMap is calling a tool, not freezing. It is fetching data or doing work, then it picks the reply back up on its own. Let it finish.
</Info>

## How long it takes

Usually a few seconds. A bigger job, like searching several sources or working through a large file, can take 10 to 30 seconds. That is normal.

If a minute passes and nothing has happened, refresh the page and ask again. See [When something breaks](/guides/10-when-something-breaks).

## What kinds of tools it uses

- **Web search** finds current information on the internet
- **File reading** opens a document you uploaded or one in your connected storage
- **Email** reads or sends mail through a connected email account
- **File creation** writes a document, spreadsheet, or note to your workspace
- **Calendar** checks or adds events when a calendar connector is on

## Reads and writes

There are two kinds of tool calls, and MindMap treats them differently.

**Reads run on their own.** Searching the web, reading a file, checking your inbox: nothing changes outside MindMap, so it goes ahead.

**Writes wait for you.** Sending an email, creating a file, changing an event: MindMap pauses and shows you exactly what it is about to do before it does it.

<Note>
Every action that changes something queues for your review first. Nothing leaves your hands without you saying yes. See [Approving what the AI does](/guides/9-approving-what-the-ai-does).
</Note>

## It asked me to approve something

That is the gate doing its job. Read what MindMap is about to do. If it looks right, click **Approve**. If you do not want it to proceed, click **Reject**, or describe what you would rather it do instead.

## Out of credits

MindMap's AI runs on credits tied to a key. Those credits can run out. You would notice:

- The assistant stops responding partway through.
- You see an error mentioning the model, credits, or a key.
- Replies that used to work now fail.

If that happens, you did nothing wrong. The key behind the AI needs more credit. Email support@madeformeai.com with what the error said.

## It is not broken

The first time people see a tool call, they often think something went wrong. It did not. MindMap is working the way it should. Give it a moment.
