North Star is the assistant on the [madeformeai.com](https://madeformeai.com) homepage.
It answers first, then asks. When you are done, you have a plain answer to what you
asked, and where it helps, a plan, a map, or a prompt pack as files you can download.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open madeformeai.com and find the chat on the homepage">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type what you are trying to get done">
    Plain words. What you do, what eats your time, what you have already tried. If
    you are not sure where to start, tap one of the quick chips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer its follow-up questions">
    Specific in, specific out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the files it hands you">
    When it has enough, it can give you a plan, a map of your situation, or a prompt
    pack as downloadable files. They are kept for 7 days, so save them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
What you type is kept so I can follow up on it; see the
[privacy policy](/legal/privacy).
</Note>

## If this fails

- It answers but never offers files -> ask for them directly, for example **give me this as a plan I can download**.
- A download link has stopped working -> files are kept for 7 days; ask again and it will rebuild them.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
