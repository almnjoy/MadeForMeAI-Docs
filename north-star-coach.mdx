The first thing on your dashboard, and the best place to start. The North Star Coach is a chat on your Home tab with one job: figure out where AI actually fits in **your** work, then hand you the files that make it happen.

## Not a sales bot

It says so on the page and it means it. The coach is not there to steer you into a purchase. It's there to help you think. You talk through what you do and what eats your time, and it does the mapping. If the honest answer is "you don't need anything built, here's a prompt that solves this," that's the answer you get.

## The frame it uses

Every task has the same shape: you're somewhere now, you want to be somewhere else, and the work is the gap. The coach digs at your gap with plain questions. What do you do? What part of it eats the most time? What have you already tried? No jargon quiz, no forms.

Stuck on where to start? The quick chips cover the common ones: scheduling chaos, leads slipping through the cracks, or just "I want to use AI but do not know where to start."

## What you walk away with

Files. This is the part that makes it different. The coach doesn't end with advice, it ends with a **portable AI harness**: your context, your workflow, and the right prompts written down as files you can hand to any AI. Paste them into Claude, ChatGPT, whatever you already use, and that AI suddenly knows your business and your gap. The harness is yours. No lock-in, take it anywhere.

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in free">
    A free account at [madeformeai.com](https://madeformeai.com) lands you on Home, coach front and center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what eats your time">
    Type it plainly or tap a chip. Talk like you'd talk to a person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it map the gap">
    Answer its questions honestly. Vague in, vague out; specific in, sharp out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the files">
    Walk away with your harness. Use it anywhere, today.
  </Step>
</Steps>
