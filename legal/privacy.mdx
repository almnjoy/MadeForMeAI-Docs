**Last updated: September 28, 2026**

This policy covers everything Quick IT Projects LLC runs for you under MadeForMeAI: the dashboard at madeformeai.com, MindMap, the free tools, North Star chat on the homepage, PocketOps, and Market Forge. It is built on a simple principle: your data is yours. Here is exactly what that means.

## What we collect

**Account information** is the email address and identity you use at our sign-in page. We use it to manage access and contact you about your account or tenant.

**Usage metadata** is basic technical logs (server uptime, error rates, response times).

**Conversations.** Conversations with North Star on the homepage and with your MindMap assistant are stored so the assistant can remember context and so we can improve the service and follow up on requests you make. You can ask us to delete them at any time at support@madeformeai.com.

## What we do NOT collect

- The content of files you upload into your workspace
- Data from connected apps (Gmail, Google Drive, and the like)
- Anything from Market Forge, which runs on your own computer with your own broker keys and does not send them to us

We do not use your conversations, memory, or files to train AI models.

## Your data stays in your tenant

MindMap runs each tenant in its own dedicated environment. Your conversations, memory, and files stay within that environment. They are not shared with other tenants and are not used for any purpose outside of running your workspace and supporting you.

## Connectors and approvals

You enable connectors yourself in the Admin marketplace, across four buckets: Apps, Tools, Skills, and Secrets. MindMap reads from a connected app only when you ask it to. Any action that writes back to a connected app queues for your approval before it runs. Credentials you add as Secrets stay in your tenant.

## Third-party AI providers

Your AI uses language models from third-party providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, or OpenRouter, depending on how your tenant is configured). When you send a message, that provider's API processes it. Each provider has its own privacy policy governing how it handles API inputs. We recommend reviewing their policies if this matters to you.

## Data deletion

If your tenant is closed, your data is held for 30 days and then permanently deleted, including all conversations, memory, and files. To have it deleted sooner, email support@madeformeai.com.

## Contact

For privacy questions or concerns: **support@madeformeai.com**
