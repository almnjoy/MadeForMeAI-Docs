A voice agent that answers or places real phone calls, books meetings, and hands off to your CRM. Approval and consent gated, with the whole call logged where the rest of your business lives.

## Both directions

Inbound, it answers your line: takes the question, books the appointment, captures the lead, and knows when to say "let me get a human." Outbound, it places the calls you keep postponing: follow-ups, confirmations, callbacks. Either way it talks like a competent front desk, not a phone tree.

## The handoff is the point

A phone call that ends in someone's memory is a phone call that didn't happen. Every call this agent takes lands in your CRM: transcript, outcome, and any record changes staged against the right client. The booking hits your real calendar. The lead becomes a real record. The phone stops being a black hole in your business data.

## Gated like everything I build

Voice is the highest-trust surface there is, so the gates are strict. Calls run with consent up front, actions the agent wants to take are approval-gated rather than fired blind, and the full log means you can always read exactly what was said and done. You get leverage without handing your phone line to a black box.

## Where it fits

Solo operators and small businesses where the owner IS the front desk: booking-heavy trades, practices, and services. The agent takes the calls that are process, so you only take the calls that are judgment.

## The stack

[Retell](https://retellai.com) for the voice layer, your calendar for booking, and your CRM for the handoff. Pairs naturally with the [OpsCanvas + Twenty CRM](/crm) build, where the call logs land on the same client records your agents already work.
