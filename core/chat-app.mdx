Chat is the conversation view of your assistant, on its own tab. You type, the reply streams in word by word, and any result the assistant produces (a chart, a document, a search, a CRM lookup) lands as a card you can open in a side panel. It is the same assistant as the Canvas, with the same memory and the same approval gate.

For a walkthrough, see [Your first chat](/guides/3-your-first-chat).

## Turning it on

Chat is a card in the marketplace. Open **Admin**, find the **Chat** card, and flip **Enable** on. It applies on your next message. There is no key to paste. If you want Chat to pull from a CRM, email, or another connector, set those up on their own cards first. See [Connect an app](/guides/5-connect-an-app).

## How it works

- Replies stream in as they are written, so you see the answer forming.
- Results render as cards (chart, document, search, lookup). Click a card to open it in a side panel for a wider view.
- Tool chips under a reply show which tools the assistant used. Follow-up chips offer a likely next step you can click instead of typing.
- Reads run on their own. Writes queue and only fire after you approve. The assistant never sends or changes anything on its own. See [Approving what the AI does](/guides/9-approving-what-the-ai-does).
- You can launch Skills from the Chat page, and you can talk to the assistant by voice.
- On a phone, Chat is the surface you land on.

## Examples

- "what's my newest lead": a read, runs on the spot
- "chart last quarter's revenue": a read, drops a chart card
- "make a one-page brief on X": a read, drops a document card
- "draft a follow-up email to them": a write, queued for your approval

## History and Clear

Your conversation stays put when you move to the Canvas or another tab and come back. History is saved in the browser you are using, so a different browser or device starts a fresh conversation. There is no synced cross-device history. Your memory is shared across all of them. See [How memory works](/understanding/how-memory-works).

<Warning>**Clear** wipes the current conversation. There is no undo and no archive of cleared conversations.</Warning>

## Chat or Canvas

Use Chat for a plain back-and-forth. Use the Canvas when you want answers as tiles you can arrange and keep. Same assistant, same keys, same memory behind both, so switch whenever you want. A write you approve in Chat is the same kind of action you would approve on the Canvas.
