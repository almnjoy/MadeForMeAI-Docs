## What the radar does now

- Pulls the day's biggest movers, then **re-verifies** every percentage against real
  price bars and the live tape - stale prints and split artifacts get dropped instead
  of shown.
- Scores each survivor **signal or noise** (0-100) with the headlines and Reddit
  chatter folded in.
- Shows live **"now versus alert"** so you can see follow-through or fade.
- Feeds hard risk gates: conviction floor, price floor, size, entries per day, total
  exposure, and a kill switch - with a trailing stop armed on every entry.
