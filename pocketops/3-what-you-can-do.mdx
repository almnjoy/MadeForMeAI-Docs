title: "What you can do from the phone"
description: "The parts of your CRM the PocketOps app covers, and the parts it does not."
---

When you finish this page you know which jobs to reach for the phone for and which still need a computer. The app builds its screens from what your CRM has, so your tabs may differ from another business's.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start on Today">
    **Today** shows a short strip of numbers, what is coming up in the next two days, and money taken today when your workspace has payment records.
    A card only shows when it has something to say. An empty pipeline shows no pipeline card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse and search any object">
    **People**, **Companies** and **Pipeline** have their own tabs. Search runs against your CRM, so a large client book searches fast.
    Your own record types (bookings, procedures, whatever your CRM was set up with) are listed too. Open **Settings**, then **Pinned objects**, to put up to three of them in the tab bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a record">
    Tap a person to see their tasks, notes, custom fields and activity in one place. Tap a related record to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit, create and delete">
    Open a record and tap a field to change it. Use the add button on a list to create a record.
    The app only shows fields your CRM will accept. If a field is missing on the phone, it is not one the CRM lets the app write.
    **Delete** appears only when your CRM role allows it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter with your saved views">
    The saved views from your CRM appear as filter chips above each list. Tap one to filter.
    A chip marked `~` is partial: the app could not translate one of that view's filters, so the list shows more than the CRM does, never less.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Actions">
    Some records show an **Actions** row with buttons, for example to send a templated email from that person's record.
    A button may ask you for a value or two on a form, then a confirm. After it runs, the result says whether it sent. "Not sent" means a step was skipped.
    The Actions row can be empty. Only workflows Dustin has wired for the app appear here. If you want one, ask.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Money, if you have it">
    A **Money** tab or money card shows only when your workspace was set up with payment records. If it is not there, it was not enabled for you.
    A booking whose deposit was sent but not paid reads **HELD**, with the checkout link one tap away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>The app also has an optional AI chat tab that needs an AI key of your own. Without a key, the tab does nothing. Every change the chat proposes waits for you to approve it before it is sent.</Note>

## What it cannot do

- No bulk actions. Changes are one record at a time.
- It cannot do more than your CRM login can. A blocked edit or delete is your CRM role, not the app.
- A workflow that only has a manual button in the CRM does not appear in Actions. It needs a version wired for the app.
- On an older CRM version, some filter chips disappear. Lists still work.
- A related record of a type the app cannot list is not offered.

## If this fails

- A tab or card you expect is missing -> open **Settings**, tap the version number five times, and read the "what this workspace supports" list. It names what the CRM would need to add.
- A list looks stale after a change on the computer -> pull down on the list to refresh.
- An action button shows "Not sent" -> the workflow ran but skipped the send. Email support@madeformeai.com with the record name and the time.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
