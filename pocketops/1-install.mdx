---
title: "Install PocketOps"
description: "Put the PocketOps for Twenty app on your phone and gather what you need to connect it."
---

When you finish this page the PocketOps for Twenty app is on your phone and you have what the next page asks for. PocketOps is the phone app for your CRM. It talks to your CRM and nowhere else.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check what phone you have">
    **iPhone:** the app is on the App Store now.
    **Android:** coming when Google finishes review. Until then, use the CRM in your phone's browser at your CRM address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the App Store listing">
    On your iPhone, open [PocketOps for Twenty on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pocketops-for-twenty/id6801201669).
    The app is free. The listing name is **PocketOps for Twenty**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Get">
    Tap **Get**, then confirm the install the way you do for any app.
    Wait for the icon to appear on your home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather what you need to connect">
    You need one of these before you open the app:
    The connect link Dustin sent you. It starts with `pocketops://connect` and fills everything in.
    Or, your CRM address (the web address you use to log in to your CRM) plus an API key from it. The next page shows where the key comes from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the app once">
    Tap the icon. The first screen shows **Explore the demo workspace** as the main button and a **Connect** button below it.
    The demo is an invented business with no connection to your data. It is fine to look around in it. Your real CRM connects on the next page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Your CRM address and API key are stored in your phone's keychain and are sent only to your CRM. The app has no server of its own and collects nothing.</Note>

Next: [Connect it to your CRM](/pocketops/2-connect).

## If this fails

- The App Store link opens but says the app is not available -> you are on an Android phone or a country store the listing does not cover. Use the CRM in your phone's browser for now.
- The app installs but the icon does not appear -> search your phone for "PocketOps". iOS sometimes places new apps in the App Library instead of the home screen.
- You do not have a connect link or an API key -> email support@madeformeai.com and ask for your PocketOps connect link.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
