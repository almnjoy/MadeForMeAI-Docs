When you finish this page the app opens on your own CRM: your people, your records, your saved views. There are two ways in. The connect link is faster. The manual path works when you do not have a link.

## Path A: the connect link Dustin sent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link on your phone">
    Tap the link in the message from Dustin. It starts with `pocketops://connect`.
    It must be opened on the phone that has the app installed. On a computer it does nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirm screen">
    The app shows the workspace name and its colour before anything is saved.
    Check the name is your business. If it is not, close the app and tell Dustin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap the confirm button. The app saves the connection and switches to it.
    You land on **Today**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Path B: your CRM address and an API key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an API key from your CRM">
    On a computer, log in to your CRM. Open **Settings**, then **API & Webhooks**, and generate an API key.
    Copy the key somewhere you can paste from on your phone. It is a long block of text.
    The key carries your own CRM role. What you can do in the app is what your CRM login can do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the app and tap Connect">
    On the first screen, tap **Connect** (the outlined button below the demo button).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your CRM address">
    Enter the web address you use to log in to your CRM, starting with `https://`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the API key">
    Paste the whole key into the key field. Then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to tell it worked

- **Today** shows a badge with your workspace name and colour at the top.
- **People** lists your real clients, and search returns names you recognise.
- If you see invented names like a made-up studio, you are still in the demo workspace. Switch below.

## Switch workspaces

The app can hold more than one workspace, and the demo is always one of them. Each has its own name and colour so you can tell whose book you are in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the workspace badge">
    On **Today**, tap the badge with the workspace name. You can also open it from the workspace card in **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the workspace">
    Choose the one you want. Every screen reloads with that workspace's data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>A connect link adds a workspace and switches to it. It does not replace one you already have.</Note>

## If this fails

- The connect link opens the App Store instead of the app -> the app is not installed on this phone yet. Install it, then tap the link again.
- The app says it cannot reach your CRM -> check the address starts with `https://` and matches the address you log in with on a computer, with nothing after the domain.
- You can see records but cannot edit or delete -> the API key's CRM role does not allow it. Email support@madeformeai.com and say which action you need.
- Still stuck: email support@madeformeai.com with a screenshot and the time it happened.
